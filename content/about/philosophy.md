---
title: Engineering Philosophy
description: How I approach building software
---

**Evidence over claims.** Public writing should match what can be verified. If a detail is not publishable, it stays unpublished rather than filled with speculation.

**Products as proof.** Capability is shown through products and case studies—what was built, why it existed, and what constraints shaped it—not through title inflation or stack lists.

**Honest documentation.** Case studies state ownership clearly. Employer work is labeled as professional practice. Independent work stands on its own when it exists.

**Systems teammates can carry.** Mentoring and small-team delivery taught that code and structure matter only if another engineer can continue the work without archaeology.

**Prefer the durable path.** In production fintech delivery, reliability and clarity outrank novelty. Complexity has to earn its place.
