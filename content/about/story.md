Kartik Juneja is a software engineer based in Karnal, HR, India.

Product work became the focus through years of shipping and maintaining production systems—not through a portfolio of demos. The interesting problems showed up in live products: unclear data, changing requirements, and systems that had to stay understandable to the next person who touched them.

This workspace exists to document that kind of work. Visitors should expect products and case studies first, engineering notes as they are written, and personal background only as context for how the work was formed.
